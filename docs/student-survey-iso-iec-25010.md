# Aguhon Student Survey: ISO/IEC 25010

## Purpose

This survey gathers students' perceptions of Aguhon's software quality. It is
adapted from the product-quality and quality-in-use characteristics shown in
ISO/IEC 25010. The items are specific to Aguhon's academic assessment, program
guidance, quiz generation, flashcards, and student account features; they are
not a validated ISO/IEC 25010 instrument.

Answer based on your experience using Aguhon. For each item, select one:

| Rating | Response |
|---|---|
| 1 | Strongly disagree |
| 2 | Disagree |
| 3 | Neither agree nor disagree |
| 4 | Agree |
| 5 | Strongly agree |
| N/A | I have not used this feature or cannot judge it |

## A. Product quality

### Functional suitability

1. To what extent do you agree that the assessment includes the questions needed to create your academic-interest and aptitude profile?
2. To what extent do you agree that the assessment results correctly reflect the answers you submitted?
3. To what extent do you agree that the program suggestions and explanations are relevant to your assessment results?
4. To what extent do you agree that Aguhon provides the features you need to review your results and explore study materials?

### Performance efficiency

5. To what extent do you agree that Aguhon's pages respond promptly when you navigate the app?
6. To what extent do you agree that your assessment is submitted and its results displayed within a reasonable time?
7. To what extent do you agree that quiz generation provides clear progress or status information while you wait?
8. To what extent do you agree that generated quizzes and flashcards become available within a reasonable time after uploading material?

### Usability

9. To what extent do you agree that it is easy to find the assessment, results, quiz, and flashcard features?
10. To what extent do you agree that the instructions make it clear how to complete the assessment and use its results?
11. To what extent do you agree that the quiz-upload and quiz-taking steps are easy to understand and complete?
12. To what extent do you agree that Aguhon clearly identifies missing or invalid information during registration and other forms and explains how to correct it?
13. To what extent do you agree that the screen layout and wording make Aguhon comfortable to use?
14. To what extent do you agree that the text, controls, and information are easy for you to see and understand on your device?

### Reliability

15. To what extent do you agree that your completed assessment results and quiz attempts remain available when you return to Aguhon?
16. If an error or interruption occurs, to what extent do you agree that Aguhon helps you continue or recover without unnecessarily repeating completed work?

### Security

17. To what extent do you agree that signing in helps ensure that only you can access your account and personal results?
18. To what extent do you agree that the consent and privacy information helps you understand how your assessment information, demographic details, and uploaded materials may be used?

## B. Quality in use

### Effectiveness

19. To what extent do you agree that Aguhon helps you complete the assessment and obtain the results you came for?
20. To what extent do you agree that the assessment results help you identify academic programs to explore further?
21. To what extent do you agree that quizzes and flashcards created from your learning materials help you review their content?

### Efficiency

22. To what extent do you agree that you can complete your intended tasks in Aguhon without unnecessary steps or effort?
23. To what extent do you agree that finding and using the app's main features takes a reasonable amount of time?

### Satisfaction

24. To what extent do you agree that Aguhon's features are useful for your academic guidance and study needs?
25. To what extent do you agree that you trust Aguhon to present your assessment results and quiz feedback as intended?
26. Overall, to what extent are you satisfied with your experience using Aguhon?

## Scope notes

- Items address student-facing features. Admin analytics and resource management
  are excluded because they are not part of the student experience.
- Maintainability, resource utilization, interoperability, and technical
  portability are not included: they are primarily internal or cannot be judged
  reliably by students through ordinary use.
- Reliability item 16 is optional when a respondent has not encountered an
  interruption; use N/A rather than asking them to guess.
- The program suggestions are exploratory guidance, not validated placement or
  admission decisions. Survey wording should not imply otherwise.
